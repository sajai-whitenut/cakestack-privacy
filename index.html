Privacy Policy

Cake Stack · Android game

Last updated: October 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Maggie ("we", "us", "our") handles information when you play the Android game Cake Stack (the "Game"). By installing or playing the Game, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Summary

  • The Game does not require an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or exact location.
  • Your game progress (best score, settings, collected stickers) is stored on your device.
  • The Game shows ads provided by Google AdMob and uses Google Firebase for analytics and crash reports. These services may collect certain device and usage data, described below.

2. Information we and our partners collect

2.1 Data stored only on your device

The Game saves your best score, sound/music/haptic settings, collected stickers, achievement counters and similar progress locally on your device. We do not receive this data on our servers. If you uninstall the Game or clear its data, this progress is deleted.

2.2 Analytics (Google Firebase Analytics)

We use Firebase Analytics to understand how the Game is used so we can improve it. Firebase may automatically collect information such as:

  • App interactions, for example game start, game over, score reached, unlocked stickers, session length;
  • Device and app information such as device model, operating system version, language, app version and approximate region (derived from IP address);
  • A randomly generated app instance identifier and, where permitted, the Android advertising ID.

2.3 Crash reports (Google Firebase Crashlytics)

If the Game crashes, Crashlytics may collect technical information such as the device model, operating system version, memory/CPU state and the crash stack trace, so we can fix bugs.

2.4 Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game displays banner, interstitial and rewarded video ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob and its partners may collect and use information such as:

  • Advertising ID, IP address and device information (model, operating system, screen size);
  • Ad interaction data (ads viewed or tapped) and approximate location based on IP address.

This information is used to deliver ads, measure ad performance, prevent fraud and, if you allow it, personalize ads. Please see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

2.5 Consent (EEA, UK and similar regions)

Where required by law, the Game shows a consent form (powered by Google's User Messaging Platform) before ads are loaded. You can accept or decline personalized ads, and you can change your choice later in the Game's settings where this option is shown.

3. How we use the information

  • To provide, operate and improve the Game;
  • To understand how players use the Game and fix bugs and crashes;
  • To show ads, which helps keep the Game free;
  • To detect fraud, abuse and technical problems;
  • To comply with legal obligations.

5. Your choices and controls

  • Reset or opt out of the advertising ID: On your Android device, go to Settings > Privacy > Ads (the path may differ by device) to delete your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization.
  • Consent choices: If you are shown a consent form, you can change your selection through the Game's privacy/consent option, when available.
  • Delete local data: Clear the Game's storage in Android settings or uninstall the Game.

6. Sharing stickers

The Game may let you share a sticker image using Android's share menu. This happens only when you choose to do it, and the image is handed to the app you pick. We do not receive or store what you share.

7. Children's privacy

The Game is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13 (or under the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Game, please contact us at the email below and we will help resolve it.

8. Data retention and security

We do not keep personal data on our own servers. Data collected through Firebase and AdMob is retained according to those services' retention settings and policies. We rely on the security measures of the platforms we use and on Android's built-in protections, but no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

9. International transfers

Google and other service providers may process data in countries other than your own. Where this applies, they use legal mechanisms and safeguards as described in their own policies.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, UK, or California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal data, to object to processing and to withdraw consent. Because we do not directly hold identifying information about you, most requests are handled through the controls in section 5 and through the relevant third-party providers. You can also contact us with questions or requests at the email below.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date. Please review it periodically. Continued use of the Game after an update means you accept the changes.
